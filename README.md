# AICountMate public exercise media

873 exercise pictures and 32 exercise videos, matched to stable catalog IDs. The app starter pack contains 50 pictures and 29 matching videos. The starter list is curated for common home and gym exercises, not a measured popularity ranking.

This repository contains public demonstration media, not the AICountMate application source or user data. No workout plans, profiles, camera captures or learned movement recordings are uploaded here.

## Files

- `thumbnails/`: WebP pictures fitted within 192 x 192 pixels, without cropping.
- Release `01.001.0018`: H.264 MP4 demonstrations fitted within 1280 x 720, 24 fps, no audio. The original clip duration is retained.
- `manifest.json`: stable IDs, URLs, exact sizes, SHA-256 checksums and per-file credits.
- `CREDITS.json` and `LICENSES/`: authors, original sources, license links, modifications and license texts.

## Reuse and credits

Pictures come from [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), published under the Unlicense. Videos come from [wger](https://wger.de/), whose individual video records identify their authors and CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Video adaptations remain CC BY-SA 4.0. Retain the per-file credits and license when redistributing or adapting them. These media licenses do not license the separate AICountMate application.

Media is a reference demonstration, not a guarantee of suitability or camera recognition accuracy. Missing or ambiguous demonstrations are omitted rather than replaced with another movement.
